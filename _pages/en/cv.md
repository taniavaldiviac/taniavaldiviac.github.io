Software and open repositories
======
More than 25 public repositories on [GitHub](https://github.com/taniavaldiviac), including bioinformatic pipelines and reproducible analyses. Highlights:
* [metabarcoding_QAQC_pipeline](https://github.com/MMARINeDNA/metabarcoding_QAQC_pipeline): collaborative quality-control pipeline for eDNA metabarcoding (MMARINeDNA network, University of Washington and NOAA).
* [eDNAoccurrenceSDM](https://github.com/taniavaldiviac/eDNAoccurrenceSDM): R tool integrating GAMs with metabarcoding occurrence data for marine species distribution models.
* [MolEcolRes_Valdivia_etal_2021](https://github.com/taniavaldiviac/MolEcolRes_Valdivia_etal_2021): reproducibility repository for Valdivia-Carrillo et al. (2021), *Molecular Ecology Resources*.
* [Metabarcoding course](https://taniavaldiviac.github.io/metabarcoding-webpage/): course materials and code (in Spanish).

Technical and computational skills
======
* **Molecular ecology:** DNA and eDNA extraction and quantification (Qubit, NanoDrop, Bioanalyzer); design and validation of species-specific qPCR (CFX96) and ddPCR (QX200) assays; library preparation for Illumina (MiSeq, NovaSeq) and Nanopore (MinION); metabarcoding (12S, 16S, 18S, COI, ITS); RAD-seq and ddRAD-seq; microsatellites; Sanger sequencing.
* **Bioinformatics and high-performance computing:** metabarcoding and population genomics; Hyak cluster (UW), NOAA NWFSC servers, and CIBNOR and UNAM infrastructure.
* **Statistical modeling:** GAMs, GAMMs, GLMs, species distribution models (including Bayesian), ecological niche modeling (Maxent) and machine learning.
* **Programming and GIS:** R, UNIX; ArcGIS, QGIS.
