Science communication
======
* **Book chapter** (in Spanish): "Phylogeographic studies: lessons for biodiversity management and conservation", in [*Biodiversity in Baja California Sur. State Study*](https://www.biodiversidad.gob.mx/region/EEB/estudios/ee_bcs), Vol. III, CONABIO (2025), pp. 43–56. An introduction to phylogeography and landscape genetics on the Baja California Peninsula, featuring the desert iguana.
* **Infographic "Underwater oases of the Mar del Plata Canyon"** (2025, in Spanish): about Schmidt Ocean Institute's Talud Continental IV expedition, which collected eDNA, sediment and plankton samples at 2,900–3,900 m depth.
* **Visit of UABC Agronomy Engineering students** to CIBNOR (November 6–7, 2025).
* **"eDNA Day"** in La Paz, Baja California Sur (2019): organized an event with posters, photos and videos on eDNA methods for studying marine fauna.
  * Poster: [Marine ecosystems of the Gulf of California](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/339162883_Ecosistemas_marinos_del_Golfo_de_California)
  * Poster: [Searching for marine species with environmental DNA](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/339159779_Buscando_especies_marinas_con_ADN_ambiental)
* **"Mutante: la ciencia cambiante" podcast** (Radio UAEM, 2024), episode "Environmental DNA in the Gulf of California" with Agustín Ávila Casanueva: the role of eDNA in vaquita research and the Gulf of California, broadcast on digital platforms and Radio UAEM.
* **Marine Ecology graduate seminar**, CICESE (April 27, 2021): [video on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/posgrado.ecologiamarina.1/videos/316292726731311).
