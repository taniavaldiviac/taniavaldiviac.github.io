Design and validation of environmental DNA detection assays for the totoaba (*Totoaba macdonaldi*), a species endemic to the Gulf of California and of high conservation concern. SECIHTI postdoctoral project at CIBNOR, in collaboration with Dr. Fausto Valenzuela Quiñonez.

**Students in this project**

* [Ángel Isidro Campa Molina](/en/students/) — MSc, CIBNOR (2026): *Development and validation of a molecular tool for detecting* Totoaba macdonaldi. [Thesis in the CIBNOR repository (in Spanish)](https://cibnor.repositorioinstitucional.mx/jspui/handle/1001/3122).
