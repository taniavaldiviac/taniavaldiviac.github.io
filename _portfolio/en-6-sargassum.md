Since June 2025 I have collaborated with the Seagrass Laboratory of the Reef Systems Academic Unit (ICMyL-UNAM, Puerto Morelos), led by Dr. Brigitta I. van Tussenbroek, on the project **"Golden tides: understanding sargassum biology and exploring its aquaculture potential"** (Frontiers of Science CBF 2023-2024-1414, SECIHTI).

My role includes co-designing sampling and analysis protocols based on environmental DNA and macroalgal swabs, metabarcoding and metagenomics, and technical advice.

**Students in this project**

* [María Fernanda Fernández González](/en/students/) — MSc, CIBNOR: *Variation of the epibiont microbiome of holopelagic* Sargassum *in the Mexican Caribbean*. Uses 16S metabarcoding to separate the contributions of the host and the ocean environment to microbiome assembly.
