Integrating eDNA metabarcoding (12S, COI), underwater visual censuses and historical records to describe fish and other communities on rocky reefs of the Gulf of California. Expeditions aboard the "Quino el Guardián" (2018–2019), supported by a National Geographic Explorer Grant and the Illumina Core-Lab program.

Related publications: [Valdivia-Carrillo et al. 2021, *Molecular Ecology Resources*](https://doi.org/10.1111/1755-0998.13375); Mac Loughlin et al. 2024 and 2025.
